## 1. What happened in FY25

I built a FY25 plan using only information available on Feb 4, 2025: 3.5% comp sales (2.0% traffic, 1.5% check), 330 openings, food at 30.3% and labor at 25.2% of revenue.

| | Plan | Actual | Variance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | 12,499 | 11,926 | (573) / -4.6% |
| Operating income | 2,025 | 1,936 | (89) / -4.4% |
| Operating margin | 16.2% | 16.2% | flat |

- **Revenue missed on traffic.** $551M of the $573M miss came from transactions: -2.9% actual against +2.0% planned. Consumers pulled back on eating out, starting in Q1 and continuing all year. Q4 transactions were still -3.2%. New restaurants and average check were close to plan.
- **Profit held up better than sales** because my cost assumptions were cautious. Food came in at 29.6% against my 30.3%, which was about $86M of real savings from menu pricing and efficiencies. G&A was $80M under plan.
- **Other operating costs hid a problem.** They were only $23M over in total, but $103M over per dollar of sales (14.7% vs 13.9%). Lower sales covered it up. The likely causes are fixed restaurant costs spread over lower sales and more marketing to win customers back.

## 2. Where FY26 is heading (H1 actual + H2 forecast)

H1 2026 came in with revenue +8.4% and comps back to +1.4%, with Q2 at +2.2% and positive traffic. Operating income was still down 11% because food (beef, freight), labor, marketing and G&A all grew faster than sales.

**Base case:** H2 comps +1.5%, which allows for the food-safety headwind management flagged in July.

| | FY25A | FY26E | FY27E |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | 11,926 | 12,932 | 14,150 |
| Growth | 5.4% | 8.4% | 9.4% |
| Operating income | 1,936 | 1,833 | 1,947 |
| Operating margin | 16.2% | 14.2% | 13.8% |
| Net income | 1,536 | 1,398 | 1,478 |

Revenue keeps growing, mostly from new restaurants (about 7 points a year). The margin keeps falling because cost inflation is running ahead of price increases.

## 3. Scenarios (FY27)

| | Downside | Base | Upside |
|---|---|---|---|
| Comp sales | 0% | 2.5% | 4.0% |
| Operating income | 1,609 | 1,947 | 2,256 |
| Operating margin | 11.8% | 13.8% | 15.6% |
| Year-end cash | 36 | 391 | 709 |

The gap between downside and upside is about $650M of operating income, and traffic and cost ratios drive most of it. In the downside case, cash falls to almost zero if buybacks stay at $1.2B, so buybacks would need to come down.

## 4. What to watch

1. **Q3 comps (late October).** My H2 assumption is +1.5%. A negative number would point toward the downside case.
2. **Food cost %.** Beef inflation and tariffs. Every 0.5 points is about $70M a year.
3. **Other operating costs / marketing.** Is the extra spending bringing customers back?
4. **Buybacks vs. cash.** Chipotle sold down about $350M of investments in H1 2026 to fund buybacks, and that can't repeat for long.
